# 🍱 Loqma (لقمة)

**Loqma** is a food-saving platform that helps cafés, bakeries, restaurants, and food stores reduce food waste by selling their unsold products at discounted prices before the end of the day. Instead of discarding fresh, surplus food, businesses can list it on Loqma, making it available for customers looking for affordable food options.

Users can explore nearby offers, view product details, prices, discounts, and availability, and choose between pickup or delivery. Loqma creates a **win-win solution** by helping businesses recover value from unsold food, giving customers access to affordable meals, and reducing food waste.

---

## 📖 Project Idea

Many food businesses end the day with fresh items that are still good today but will spoil if kept until tomorrow. Loqma connects these businesses with users who are looking for good food at affordable prices, so the food is sold on the same day instead of being thrown away.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- **Help users save money** by offering good food at lower prices.
- **Help businesses benefit** from unsold products instead of throwing them away.
- **Reduce food waste** and support a more sustainable community.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview

### 1. Real-Time Digital Ecosystem
- Mobile app (iOS & Android) that uses **GPS** to show the nearest live offers.
- **Push notifications** for new offers and low-stock alerts.

### 2. Flexible Pickup & Delivery
- Customers can choose **Pickup** (cheaper) or **Delivery**.
- **Interactive maps** show distance, pickup time, and directions.

### 3. Discount Marketplace & Fast Payment
- Items shown with **original price**, **discount %**, and **remaining quantity**.
- **Multiple secure payment options** for instant booking and confirmation.

---

## ⚠️ Challenges & Solutions

| # | Challenge | Solution |
|---|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Users may worry about food quality | Show clear photos, ingredients, and prices, and mark items as "Fresh Today" |
| 2 | Quantities change quickly during the day | Update stock in real time and send alerts to users |
| 3 | Shops need a quick way to post offers | Provide a simple and fast upload screen for merchants |
| 4 | Mostly pickup, with limited delivery | Let users choose between pickup and delivery |
